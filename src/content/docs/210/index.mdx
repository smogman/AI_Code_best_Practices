When Claude works fast, the review window shrinks. A session that produces 14 commits in 45 minutes gives you less than 3 minutes per commit. Without the right habits, you lose track of what changed, why it changed, and whether it was what you intended.

This course covers the four habits that practitioners have converged on after months of production use with Claude Code.

## Lessons

1. [Feature Branches and Blast Radius](/AI_Code_best_Practices/210/feature-branches/): every Claude session starts on a branch, every mistake is contained
2. [Atomic Commits and Conventional Commits](/AI_Code_best_Practices/210/commit-discipline/): one logical change per commit, every commit tells you why
3. [The Pre-Push Review](/AI_Code_best_Practices/210/review-and-pr/): the human checkpoint before Claude's work reaches shared history

## Prerequisites

Complete 201. You should be running Claude on real projects. These habits assume you are already past the basics and are developing a production workflow.

## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Contain the blast radius of any session that goes wrong
- Read the git log and understand what changed and why without asking
- Catch scope creep and unexpected changes before they reach main
- Write PRs that reviewers can evaluate without asking questions
